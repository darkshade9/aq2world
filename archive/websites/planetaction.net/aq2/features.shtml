AQ2 Features

  • Incredibly faced-paced gameplay, keeping you on your toes. You can move round maps with great haste or take a more stealthy approach, whichever suits your style. Either way, you'll need to watch out for opponents doing the same.
  • A varied and balanced range of weapons, no one weapon dominates the game and all can kill:

    • SOG 37 Combat Knife - your basic weak knife, no? Not at all, this baby can kill in one hit, get up close and they're done for. If you don't like the sound of that, throw it at your target. Throwing knives are lethal and a deadly skill to master.
    • Heckler & Koch MK23 - this 9mm semi-automatic pistol is your standard sidearm. Can be useful and deadly both up close and at long range. This gun is your saviour when your main weapon runs out of ammo.
    • Akimbo MK23's - style incarnate, kill your enemies in true John Woo fashion. Fast, accurate and deadly, you can also get more ammo for these by picking up other fallen players' guns. Look out for the stylish reload animation.
    • H&K Mp5K - this 9mm submachinegun boasts an impressive rate of fire. You can use it to pick off enemies at a distance or get up close and gun them down. Extremely inaccurate when moving, crouching or using a lasersight increases the accuracy no-end.
    • 12 Gauge Handcannon - take your standard 12 gauge double-barrel hunting shotgun, saw the barrels off, file down to a pistol hilt and you have this killer of a weapon. At close range you'll leave your enemy as nothing more than a collection of scattered body parts.
    • H&K Beneli Super 90 M3 - standard issue to police forces all over the world and for good reason. This pump action shotgun will demolish enemies up close with it's high damage and fast rate of fire. At longer ranges you can whittle them down before rushing in to finish.
    • SIG Sauer SSG 3000 - a lethal weapon in the hands of the master, a shot to the stomach, head or unprotected chest will plain demolish your opponent, and anything else will leave them in serious need of help.
    • Colt 727 Assault Rifle (aka M4) - this military-issue hardware is a serious beast. Up close it's sheer damage and speed will shred your opponent's flesh like paper. At longer ranges switch to three round burst mode and pick them off.
    • M26 Fragmentation Grenade - the enemy all clumped together in one spot and not coming out? Teach them the error of their ways with this pineapple of death. Set the fuse, pull the pin, toss it their way and watch as the problem disappears in a cloud of smoke and shrapnel.

  • A number of useful items commonly found in action movies

    • Kevlar Vest - wear one of these and all shots to your chest area will be stopped dead, greatly reducing the damage the need to bandage. Watch out for the armor-piercing shots of the SSG though, they'll rip right through your vest...
    • Bandolier - it could be well advised to wear one of these, you'll have twice the ammo capacity and be able to carry an extra special weapon. The built-in IR vision will have your enemies lighting up like Christmas trees on even the darkest of maps. Also, you'll be granted with a grenade if you start with this in a teamplay game.
    • Laser Sight - attach this to your MK23, MP5 or M4 and your shots will go a lot closer to where you aim. You'll be free to move a lot more when shooting without fear of your shots going amiss.
    • Stealth Slippers - slip on a pair of these and play a very different game indeed. With the sound of your footsteps masked, hide in the shadows and move under cover before taking your shot, your opponent will never know what hit them. Once your opponent knows you're packing these they'll be afraid to even turn the corner.
    • Silencer - attach one of these to your MP5, MK23 or SSG and your weapon will produce much less sound and no muzzleflare. In deep shadow this is invaluable, your target will be under fire but won't have a clue where is safe to run and hide.
  • Hundreds of detailed, realistic maps available at the Map Depot.
  • Lots of brilliant new skins and models for both players and weapons available at the Actor's Guild, make Action look the way you want.
  • 2 exciting modes of play. Deathmatch is Action at it's best, every shot is crucial, live on your wits, stealth and speed, for death is just round the next corner. If you prefer a more strategical game, Teamplay is for you, choose your weapon and item and seek to wipe out the other team by working as a group.
