"Is there an Action Quake 3?" | June 16, 2001 /Mloclam
While the A Team is not making an Action Quake 3 of any sorts, their attentions of course being focused on Action Unreal Tournament, there is another group out there who are striving to bring the Action Quake 2 feel to Quake 3. I'll leave the explanation up to SpecOps of the Reaction Quake 3 team, though:

"A RETURN TO ACTION

From the ashes of Quake 2, a legendary modification was born. Perhaps the forefather of "realism" mods, it left us with definitive gameplay and action. Now, time has passed... but the legend is not forgotten. It rises again and makes its return to Quake 3 Arena.

Reaction Quake 3 ("Reaction") is a direct port of the famed Action Quake 2 ("Action") modification for Quake 3. All the original weapons and gameplay features will return. The Reaction team is dedicated to matching Action's original physics and creating an authentic gaming experience. Some of Action's most popular maps are also being ported. However, the similarities end there. Utilizing Quake 3's improved graphics capabilities, the Reaction team intends to raise the level on visual aesthetics and environmental depth. The end result is a new and revitalized modification that will please the gaming veteran and rookie in you.

Reaction Quake 3 is a modification driven by members of the Action Quake 2 community. Although there is a dedicated team committed to its development, in the spirit of Action's community approach, outside project contributions are encouraged. For the latest in Reaction news, visit the official website at http://www.telefragged.com/reaction"

I should stress that this is not a project by the A Team, but oddjob has donated the smashing AHL Handcannon model to RQ3 so they're all least having some part in it. I'm personally really looking forward to Reaction, the early version I played already showed great promise and a huge amount of work has happened since then, be sure to check the website out.

On a semi-related note, I recently spoke to Black Monk, international AQ2 man of mystery, about the forthcoming offical AQ2 client pack and a few other matters, I'll post my little report on his actions after I finish writing it up.

Haven O' Frags update + AHL Armoury News | May 6, 2001 /rogue
MonArcH of the HoF staff sends the following news:

Today at Haven 'o Frags, we've posted some more preview pics of the models that will be included with the upcoming release of the Armoury. The Armoury is a powerful and easy to use program that allows AHL'ers to modify how their weapons look in-game through a comprehensive graphical interface. This means no annoying manual file movement and modification is needed. The Armoury will come with replacements for each weapon right along with the release. Afterwards, HoF will be releasing more and more replacements for download as they are submitted by skilled modelers and skinners! Stay tuned!
