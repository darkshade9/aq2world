 Unofficial Mods (Because there are no Official ones yet =)
Action Quake: Espionage - Introduces many new excellent gametypes such as Assassinate The Leader and Capture The Briefcase. Also home to several files which are crucial to AQ2 these days. Check this one out
The ProMagnon Custom AQ2 Mod - Single player Action quake, normal Q2 levels and objectives but with AQ2 damage and weapons. Give a Stroggos a nice juicy headshot.
Action Quake Gangsters! - Play as a gangster, protect your boss, do it as a team.
3-Team Action Quake - Pretty self explanatory, instead of 2 teams you get 3. This also adds the option of having a kevlar helmet as your item.
