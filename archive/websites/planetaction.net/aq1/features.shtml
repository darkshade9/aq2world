    AQ1 Features

    If you have played Action Quake2, then you will be familiar with the basic premise, but to just outline the basics for the rest of you....

    • Cool weapons
    • Realoading
    • Revised damage system, you bleed
    • Locational Damage, head shots kill
    • Variable accuracy
    • Unique weapon system
    • Unique item system
    • Realistic falling damage

    Speaking of Weapons, here they are.

    SOG Desert Dagger
    This double edged dager has a serrated edge as well as a straight edge to inflict the most damage. It can be used to slash at, or to throw at your enemy. You start with one knife by default. Good for extreme close quarters fighting, as well as long range fighting.

    Beretta 93r
    Your primary weapon in the Beretta 93r machine pistol. This stock pistol is always on you, with the option of semi-automatic or 3 round burst fire modes. It takes a 24 round clip, which you have one extra when you start. This is an all around good weapon, and can easily kill with one hit.

    Akimbo Beretta 92fs
    One of the unique weapons available to you, this is actually 2 pistols fired together rapidly. With a 16 round clip in each pistol, you have a total of 32 bullets to fire. 2 bullets are fired everytime you press the attack button. Good in medium to close range situations

    Remington 870 Marine version .12GA shotgun
    This stainless steel shotgun is extremely durable and world renown for its design. Holding 6 rounds in a tubular magazine, it can fire with the utmost reliability in extreme situations. This is another unique weapon, and is good for medium to close range fights.

    Over and under .12GA shotgun
    A basic clay shooting shotgun, once a gentlemens gun, this one has had the barrels sawed off to the forestock, and the stock cut down to a pistol grip. Extremely conpact and powerful, this gun holds 2 shells at once, and fires both of them with a single pull of the trigger. Due to the over and under design there is not nearly as much horizontal spread as a traditional sawed off shotgun; instead the spread is more pronounced in the vertical direction. This unique weapon is good in close range.

    Heckler & Koch MP10SD
    A fully silenced light assault rifle, that fires 10mm bullets in several fire modes. You have the ability to fire in semi-automatic, 3 round burst mode, and fully-automatic. You can carry up to 2 extra 30 round stick clips. This unique weapon is good for close and medium range fights, and for people that want to hide in the shadows and try to hunt with stealth.

    Or....

    Heckler & Koch UMP45
    Universal Machine Pistol is what UMP stands for; this ultra modern MP5K-PDW replacement fires the powerful .45 ACP round in semi-auto, 2 round busrt, and full auto. It comes standard with either 25 or 10 round stick clips, but for Action it uses custom 30 round clips manufactured by Ace12GA Co. This unique weapon is good for close and medium range fights, and easily accepts a laser sight or a silencer.

    Heckler & Koch G36
    The top of the line, brand new medium assault rifle from Heckler and Koch. This rifle was designed for the shooter, by the shooter. Built in dual sights allow quick sighting with good long range shooting, due to the built in optical sights, which allow you to zoom in once. This unique weapon fires the 5.56mm NATO round from a 30 round magazine in semi-automatic, 3 round burst, and fully-automatic. You can carry 1 extra clip. Good medium and long range weapon.

    Colt M4A1
    This is the sniper rifle conversion to the popular M4 medium assault rifle, that only fires in semi-automatic mode due to vibrational damage that can occur to the scope in other fire modes. A very accurate rifle that allows you to zoom up to 3X. Amunition is stored in 18 round clips for easy and fast loading. You can carry up 1 extra clip. Very good for long range fights, but can be used when not zoomed in, with a loss of accuracy.
